# Evidence reviewed before v5 rules — 29 September 2026

This is a focused evidence review, not a systematic review or proof of individual optimality. User-requested practical ranges are programming choices; warning thresholds are transparent pilot heuristics.

| Primary source | Finding and limitation | Application |
|---|---|---|
| [Robinson et al. 2024](https://rke.abertay.ac.uk/en/publications/exploring-the-dose-response-relationship-between-estimated-resist/) | Exploratory meta-regressions associate lower estimated RIR with more hypertrophy; exact dose response remains uncertain, with RIR inferred from study descriptions. | Make near-failure work available; do not claim a precise optimal RIR by experience from this study. |
| [Refalo et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9935748/) | Momentary failure was not demonstrably superior to non-failure; definitions and protocols varied. | RIR0 permitted selectively, never mandatory for every set. |
| [Singer et al. 2024](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sports-and-active-living/articles/10.3389/fspor.2024.1429789/full) | Small potential benefit of longer vs short rests, heterogeneous short studies; additional hypertrophy benefit beyond90s remains equivocal. | 90–240s are practical recovery options, not proven universal optimum or a rest-by-experience law. |
| [Pelland et al. 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-025-02344-w) | Volume showed positive dose response with diminishing returns. Direct/indirect treatment matters. Independent frequency effect on hypertrophy compatible with negligible effects. Abstract publicly available; full paper paywalled. | Keep direct/secondary counts separate, allow individualizable volume, use frequency to distribute work. No claim that intensity replaces volume. |

## Explicit heuristics (not physiological cutoffs)

Beginner starts3–4RIR,2 selective,0–1 invalid in this pilot. Experienced0–2 usable; uncertain self-estimation raises review warnings. Stable machine does not mean low fatigue (hack/press remain high-fatigue). Failure-suitability categories are qualitative. Warn >4 failure sets/session or >50%, >=3 high-fatigue failure sets, >14session sets with >=3failure sets; >18direct muscle sets with failure. Flag technical failure, short recovery, overly low accessory reps, beginner variation/top-backoff. No quotas requiring outputs to hit failure.

Rest references90s accessories,120s compounds,150s high-fatigue RIR0–1; bounds60–240. Reps5–15 compounds,8–20 dynamic abdomen/accessories,6–15 control. These are pilot guardrails, not evidence-derived precise thresholds. Time model retains v4 margins and counts per-set prescription/each side. Volume targets guide, not guarantee recovery. No forced reps, automatic weights, deloads, mesocycles or adaptive routine changes.
